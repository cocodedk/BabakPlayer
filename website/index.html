Android companion app for BabakCast

Play split media like one continuous story.

BabakPlayer imports audio and video parts shared from messaging apps and plays them one after another as one playlist.

BabakCast -> BabakPlayer

1. BabakCast creates parts

BabakCast splits large media files into smaller parts you can send through messaging apps.

2. Share to BabakPlayer

Share one or many files from WhatsApp, or from any app with a Share button, and choose BabakPlayer.

3. Continuous playback

BabakPlayer starts playing as soon as the import finishes and goes through all the parts, one after another.

Import supported audio and video parts shared from BabakCast.

Why BabakPlayer

Parts in the right order

Files shared with a caption go into one playlist, in order by file name (part2 before part10). Files shared without a caption play in the order they were shared.

Continuous playback

Playback moves through all the parts by itself. You can pause, skip to the next or previous file, and move forward or backward within a file.

Remove playlists

Remove a playlist from BabakPlayer without deleting your original files. Private copies that BabakPlayer made stay until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Private and local

No account is required. Files stay in their original locations where possible; otherwise BabakPlayer keeps a private copy.

Google Cast in the GitHub version

Cast audio to compatible Nest speakers, or audio and video to compatible Chromecast devices on your network. The F-Droid version does not include Cast.

How to install the app (APK)

1

Download the installation file

Use the download link above, or get the Android installation file (.apk) from GitHub Releases.

2

Allow installation

If Android blocks the installation, open the settings it offers and turn on "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager. The wording varies by device.

3

Install the app

Open the downloaded .apk file and tap "Install".

4

Open BabakPlayer

Open BabakPlayer from your app list, tap + and choose your files. The app creates a playlist and starts playback.
