BabakPlayer is a companion app for BabakCast, the app that splits large media into parts you can send through messaging apps. BabakPlayer imports those parts when you share them to it from other apps, such as WhatsApp, and plays them one after another as one playlist.

Parts shared with a caption go into one playlist named after the caption, in order by file name (part2 before part10). Parts shared without a caption play in the order they were shared.

It supports common audio and video formats, including mp3, mp4, mkv, mov, and webm. Whether a file plays can also depend on your device. If a file cannot be played, it is marked as failed and playback continues with the next file, if there is one.

The app is available in English, Persian, and Danish, with the Persian layout running right-to-left automatically.

Files stay where they are when Android allows lasting access to them. Otherwise the app saves a private copy on your phone. Removing a playlist does not delete your media files, and private copies stay until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The app has no account, cloud sync, or backend service, and playing files on your phone needs no internet connection.
